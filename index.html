Professional profile

Helping teams grow, work better, and stay connected

I’m an HR and People professional with experience supporting growing technology organizations and the people behind them. I’ve worked closely with Engineering, Product, Operations, and Go to Market teams, helping leaders navigate people challenges while building processes that make day to day work easier.

My experience spans employee relations, manager support, employee lifecycle, HR operations, performance, talent, compensation, and compliance. I’ve worked across organizations of different sizes and supported teams across North America, Europe, and APAC, which has taught me how to adapt my approach to what each organization and team actually needs.

My approach is practical. I like giving managers the tools and confidence to handle people situations well, while making sure the processes behind them are simple, consistent, and easy to use. Good HR should make things easier for managers and employees, not add another layer of complexity.
