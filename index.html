Professional profile

A people partner built for scale

Strategic HR Business Partner with experience supporting global SaaS, software engineering and technology organizations through periods of rapid growth and transformation.

I have spent my career close to the teams that build the product — advising managers on employee relations and high-risk performance cases, running performance and compensation cycles, and keeping employee lifecycle operations accurate across North America, Europe and APAC. Along the way I have owned end-to-end payroll for a 400-plus employee workforce and led talent reviews for hundreds of engineers and technical specialists.

My approach is practical: give managers the language and the frameworks to handle people decisions themselves, then remove the operational friction underneath them. That means standardized documentation, clean HRIS data, defensible compliance, and processes that still work when the headcount doubles.
